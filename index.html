Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the FitCity mobile application for Android and iOS, together with any related services operated by "NEXTBRAIN" MCHJ (collectively, the "Application"). "NEXTBRAIN" MCHJ is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

FitCity is the membership application of the FitCity network of sports clubs. It lets members manage their subscription, book and follow their training plan, contact their trainer, find club branches, and track their daily physical activity.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Phone number, used to create and sign in to your account
  • First name, last name, date of birth and gender
  • Profile photo, if you choose to upload one
  • A facial photograph, if you choose to enable Face ID entry at club turnstiles. This is optional; you can use the club without it
  • Messages you send to your trainer or to club support
  • Feedback and ratings you submit

Information collected automatically

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Mobile operating system and device model
  • Screens of the Application you visit, and the date and time of your visit
  • Your approximate or precise location, only while you are using the branch map to find a nearby club, and only if you grant location permission. Location is not collected in the background
  • Club visit history and subscription and payment records

Health and Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, the Application reads a limited set of health and fitness data from Health Connect on Android and from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS, in order to show you your own activity inside the Application.

Data type Access Why it is needed
Steps Read To display your daily step count, your progress toward your daily step goal, and your weekly and monthly step history
Active calories burned Read To display the calories burned through activity on the activity card and in your activity statistics
Exercise sessions Read To calculate and display your daily active minutes
Hydration Read and write To display how much water you drank today and to save the amount you enter in the Application, so it stays in sync with your other health apps

Health data stays on your device. The health and fitness data listed above is read on your device and displayed to you only. It is not transmitted to the Service Provider's servers, is not shared with any third party, is not sold, and is not used for advertising, marketing, or any form of profiling. Hydration is the only data type the Application writes, and it is written back to Health Connect or Apple Health on your own device.

Your daily step goal, your daily water goal and the water amounts you enter are stored locally on your device so that the Application works when a health provider is unavailable.

How to withdraw access to health data

You can revoke access at any time, without affecting the rest of the Application:

  • Android: open the Health Connect app, go to App permissions, select FitCity, and turn off the permissions or remove all access
  • iOS: open Settings, go to Health, then Data Access & Devices, select FitCity, and turn off the categories

When access is revoked, the Application stops reading health data immediately. Because health data is never uploaded, there is nothing for the Service Provider to delete on its side. Any hydration records the Application wrote remain in Health Connect or Apple Health, where you can delete them yourself. Uninstalling the Application removes the goals and water amounts stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and your club membership
  • To grant access to club branches and, if enabled, Face ID entry
  • To show your training plan, schedule and trainer
  • To process subscription payments and keep payment history

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all further collection of information by uninstalling the Application. You can also turn off individual permissions (health, location, camera, microphone, notifications) in your device settings at any time; the rest of the Application will continue to work.

Uninstalling stops the Application from collecting data from your device, but it does not automatically delete information that has already been transmitted to the Service Provider. To request deletion of your account and personal data, contact the Service Provider using the details below.

Data Retention

  • Health and fitness data: never stored on the Service Provider's servers. Goals and water amounts are stored on your device until you uninstall the Application or clear its data
  • Account and profile data: retained for the duration of your membership plus 12 months, unless longer retention is required by law
  • Facial photograph for Face ID entry: retained until you disable Face ID entry or delete your account
  • Automatically collected data: retained for up to 24 months from collection, unless longer retention is required for legal compliance
  • Aggregated and anonymized data: retained indefinitely, as it no longer identifies you
